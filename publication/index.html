Research experience

  • Comparative genomics of lytic S. aureus bacteriophages — independent research, first author 2025 – 2026

    Curated 22 complete lytic phage genomes from NCBI GenBank against predefined inclusion criteria, quantified intergenomic similarity with VIRIDIC, and extracted features with Biopython and pandas. Annotated lysis (endolysin, holin) and host-recognition (tail fibre/RBP) modules with InterPro, resolved misannotated endolysins by tBLASTn, and identified two mechanisms of gene interruption: HNH-endonuclease insertion and group I intron splitting. Re-annotation with Pharokka and Phold raised RBP detection from 14/22 to 20/22 genomes. Inferred a maximum-likelihood phylogeny from the large terminase subunit (MAFFT L-INS-i; MEGA 12.1.2, LG+G+I, 1000 bootstraps) and predicted tRNAs de novo with tRNAscan-SE, finding them in 18/22 genomes against 5 annotated in GenBank. Supervised by N. I. Alifiyah, Universitas Terbuka.

  • Containerised comparative phage-genomics pipeline — independent development 2025 – 2026

    Built a multi-service Docker Compose stack (WSL2, GPU-enabled) with isolated conda environments per tool, orchestrating Pharokka, tRNAscan-SE, DefenseFinder/AntiDefenseFinder and custom Python modules. Pinned tool and database versions and recorded provenance in every output; validated modules with unit tests, synthetic smoke tests and literature-anchored control cases. Modelled receptor-binding and depolymerase domains with AlphaFold/ColabFold and checked folds with Foldseek and Dali.

  • Literature review: phage therapy against resistant S. aureus 2024 – 2025

    Structured review across PubMed, Scopus and ScienceDirect linking mecA/PBP2a and OatA resistance mechanisms to phage-efficacy outcomes and paradoxical biofilm responses. Published in Jurnal Biologi Tropis, 2025.

  • Undergraduate laboratory practicals — Universitas Terbuka 2022 – 2024

    Aseptic technique and sterilisation, culture-media preparation, isolation by spread, pour and streak plating, Gram staining and microscopy, antimicrobial inhibition-zone assays, plant DNA isolation and spectrophotometry.
